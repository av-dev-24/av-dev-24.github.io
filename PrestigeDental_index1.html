Our story

A Makati practice built on a simple idea: slow down the visit, sharpen the outcome.

Prestige Dental Clinic of the Philippines opened its first chair on Ayala Avenue in 2010, founded by Dr. Antonio Reyes after a decade practicing restorative dentistry in Metro Manila and abroad. The clinic grew on referrals rather than advertising — patients who wanted a dentist who explained the "why" behind a treatment plan, not just the "what."

Today the practice runs two clinics, Makati and Bonifacio Global City, with a resident team covering general, cosmetic, orthodontic and surgical care under one roof, so a treatment plan rarely means a referral out the door.

"We don't measure a good appointment by how quickly it ended. We measure it by whether the patient understood their own mouth a little better than when they walked in." — Dr. Antonio Reyes, DMD, Founder & Chief Dentist

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you accept HMOs or dental insurance?

We're accredited with most major HMOs in the Philippines for basic dental benefits. Bring your HMO card to your appointment and our front desk will verify coverage before treatment starts. For non-accredited plans, we issue official receipts and a claim form for reimbursement.

Can I walk in, or do I need an appointment?

Appointments are strongly recommended so we can hold a full time slot for your exam. That said, both branches keep same-day emergency slots open for dental pain, swelling or a broken tooth — call ahead and we'll fit you in.

I live abroad — can you coordinate a visit around my trip home?

Yes. Many of our orthodontic and implant patients are based overseas. Tell us your travel dates when booking and we'll sequence your treatment plan — including any lab work — around the days you're in Manila.

Do you offer payment plans for larger treatments?

Implants, full-mouth cases and orthodontic treatment can be split into staged payments tied to treatment milestones. Your dentist will lay out the full schedule in writing before you commit to anything.

What should I bring to my first visit?

A valid ID, your HMO card if applicable, and any recent X-rays or dental records you have on hand. If you're coming in for pain or trauma, just come as you are — paperwork can wait.
